## 🎯 Before You Start

### Documents You'll Need

Prepare these documents BEFORE starting (based on your business type):

#### For Individual/Proprietorship:
- ✅ PAN Card (mandatory)
- ✅ Aadhaar Card (for identity verification)
- ✅ Bank Account Statement (last 3 months)
- ✅ Cancelled Cheque or Bank Passbook (for account verification)
- ✅ Proof of Business Address (if different from residential)
- ✅ GST Certificate (if registered)

#### For Private Limited Company:
- ✅ Company PAN Card
- ✅ Certificate of Incorporation
- ✅ Memorandum of Association (MOA)
- ✅ Articles of Association (AOA)
- ✅ Board Resolution for authorized signatory
- ✅ Director's PAN Card
- ✅ Director's Aadhaar Card
- ✅ Company Bank Account Statement
- ✅ GST Certificate (if registered)

#### For LLP/Partnership:
- ✅ LLP/Partnership PAN Card
- ✅ LLP Agreement/Partnership Deed
- ✅ Certificate of Incorporation/Registration
- ✅ Partner's PAN Card
- ✅ Partner's Aadhaar Card
- ✅ Bank Account Statement
- ✅ GST Certificate (if registered)

### Important Tips:
- 📸 Scan documents in **high quality** (PDF format preferred)
- 📄 All documents should be **clear and legible**
- ✅ Documents should be **valid and not expired**
- 🔒 Keep all documents under **5MB per file**

## 🚀 Account Creation

### Step 1: Visit Razorpay Website

1. Go to https://razorpay.com
2. Click **"Sign Up"** button (top-right corner)
3. Or directly go to: https://dashboard.razorpay.com/signup

### Step 2: Enter Basic Details

**Form Fields:**

1. **Email Address**
   - Enter your business email (e.g., `founder@reframeai.com`)
   - ⚠️ Avoid personal emails like Gmail (use business domain if possible)
   - ✅ Use an email you actively monitor

2. **Mobile Number**
   - Enter your business mobile number
   - This will receive OTP and transaction alerts
   - Format: +91-XXXXXXXXXX

3. **Password**
   - Create a strong password (8+ characters)
   - Include uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols
   - Example: `ReframeAI@2025!`

4. **Verify OTP**
   - Check your email for OTP
   - Check your mobile for SMS OTP
   - Enter both OTPs to verify

**Expected Result:** Account created, redirected to dashboard

### Step 3: Email and Mobile Verification

1. Click on verification link sent to email
2. Verify mobile number via OTP
3. Both must be verified before proceeding

## 🏢 Business Details Selection

This is **CRITICAL** for approval. Choose carefully based on your project.

### Step 1: Navigate to Account & Settings

1. Log in to Razorpay Dashboard
2. Click on **"Settings"** (gear icon)
3. Go to **"Account & Settings"** or **"Business Settings"**

### Step 2: Select Business Type

**For Reframe AI, recommended options:**

#### Option 1: **Private Limited Company** (RECOMMENDED if incorporated)
**Select if:**
- ✅ You have a registered Pvt Ltd company
- ✅ You want higher transaction limits
- ✅ You want better credibility
- ✅ You're serious about scaling

**Advantages:**
- Higher approval rate
- Higher transaction limits (₹2L-₹10L per transaction)
- Better customer trust
- Easier to raise funding later

#### Option 2: **LLP (Limited Liability Partnership)**
**Select if:**
- ✅ You have an LLP registration
- ✅ Multiple partners involved
- ✅ Less compliance than Pvt Ltd

#### Option 3: **Proprietorship/Individual**
**Select if:**
- ✅ No formal business registration yet
- ✅ Running as freelancer/individual
- ✅ Want to start quickly

**Disadvantages:**
- Lower transaction limits (₹50K-₹1L per transaction)
- Higher scrutiny during KYC
- May face issues with scalability

**⚠️ IMPORTANT:** If you're unsure, **Proprietorship** is easiest to start with. You can upgrade later.

### Step 3: Select Business Category

**For Reframe AI video editing SaaS:**

1. **Primary Category:**
   - ✅ **"IT & Software"** (RECOMMENDED)
   - Or **"Media & Entertainment"**
   - Or **"Services"**

2. **Sub-Category:**
   - ✅ **"SaaS Platform"** (BEST MATCH)
   - Or **"Software Development"**
   - Or **"Video Editing Services"**
   - Or **"Technology Services"**

3. **Business Model:**
   - ✅ **"Subscription-based services"**
   - Or **"Software as a Service (SaaS)"**

**Why this matters:**
- Razorpay uses this to assess risk
- Wrong category can lead to rejection
- Affects transaction limits and fees

### Step 4: Describe Your Business

**Business Description Field:**

Write a clear, professional description. Here's a template for Reframe AI:

```
Reframe AI is a cloud-based video editing and repurposing platform that uses
artificial intelligence to help content creators automatically convert long-form
videos into short, engaging clips for social media platforms.

Our SaaS platform offers:
- AI-powered video analysis and clipping
- Automated subtitle generation
- Multi-platform optimization (YouTube, Instagram, TikTok)
- Cloud-based video processing
- Subscription-based pricing (Free, Starter, Professional plans)

Target customers: Content creators, digital marketers, social media managers,
YouTubers, podcasters, and businesses looking to repurpose video content.

Pricing: Monthly and yearly subscription plans ranging from ₹2,400/month to
₹6,560/month (or $29-$79/month for international customers).

Website: [your-website-url]
```

**Tips for description:**
- ✅ Be clear and specific
- ✅ Mention it's a SaaS/subscription model
- ✅ Explain the value proposition
- ✅ Mention target customers
- ✅ Include pricing range
- ⚠️ Avoid vague descriptions
- ⚠️ Don't mention anything that might be flagged (crypto, gambling, adult content)

## 📝 Filling Business Information

### Step 1: Company/Business Name

**What to enter:**
- Legal name as per registration documents
- Example: `Reframe AI Technologies Private Limited`
- Or (for proprietorship): `Your Name` or `Your Name - Reframe AI`

**Tips:**
- ✅ Must match PAN Card/Incorporation Certificate
- ⚠️ Don't use just brand name if different from legal name

### Step 2: Business PAN

**What to enter:**
- Company PAN (for Pvt Ltd/LLP)
- Individual PAN (for Proprietorship)
- Format: `AAAAA9999A`

**Verification:**
- Razorpay will verify PAN with Income Tax database
- Ensure name on PAN matches exactly

### Step 3: GSTIN (Optional but Recommended)

**What to enter:**
- Your 15-digit GST number if registered
- Format: `22AAAAA0000A1Z5`
- Leave blank if not registered

**Benefits of providing GST:**
- ✅ Faster approval
- ✅ Higher trust score
- ✅ Automatic TDS handling

### Step 4: Business Address

**What to enter:**
```
Building/Office: 301, Tech Tower
Street: MG Road
City: Bangalore
State: Karnataka
PIN: 560001
```

**Tips:**
- ✅ Must match address proof document
- ✅ Use complete address (not just "Bangalore")
- ✅ If home-based, use residential address

### Step 5: Website/App Information

**What to enter:**
- **Website URL:** `https://reframeai.com` (or your domain)
- **App Store Link:** If you have mobile app
- **Social Media:** LinkedIn, Twitter profiles

**If you don't have a website yet:**
- Use your deployed app URL (Firebase/Vercel URL)
- Or provide GitHub repository (if open source)
- Or create a simple landing page

**Tips:**
- ✅ Website should clearly show what you do
- ✅ Should have contact information
- ✅ Should mention pricing/plans
- ⚠️ Website downtime can delay approval

### Step 6: Bank Account Details

**What to enter:**
- **Account Holder Name:** Must match business name
- **Account Number:** Your business current/savings account
- **IFSC Code:** Bank IFSC code
- **Bank Name:** Full bank name
- **Branch:** Branch name/location

**Tips:**
- ✅ For Pvt Ltd: Use company current account
- ✅ For Proprietorship: Can use personal savings account (with same name as PAN)
- ✅ Upload cancelled cheque to auto-fill details
- ⚠️ Account name mismatch is #1 rejection reason

### Step 7: Business Type and Nature

**Select:**
- **Business Type:** Private Limited / LLP / Proprietorship
- **Business Nature:** IT & Software / SaaS
- **Category:** Services
- **Sub-category:** Software as a Service

### Step 8: Transaction Volume Estimate

**What to enter:**
- **Expected Monthly Volume:** Be realistic
  - Starting: ₹50,000 - ₹1,00,000
  - After 6 months: ₹5,00,000 - ₹10,00,000
  - After 1 year: ₹10,00,000 - ₹50,00,000

- **Average Transaction Size:**
  - ₹2,400 (monthly starter plan)
  - ₹6,560 (monthly professional plan)

**Tips:**
- ✅ Be realistic, not overly optimistic
- ✅ You can increase limits later
- ⚠️ Very high estimates may trigger extra verification

## 📤 KYC Submission Process

### Step 1: Navigate to KYC Section

1. Go to Razorpay Dashboard
2. Click **"Settings"** → **"Account & Settings"**
3. Look for **"KYC Status"** or **"Complete KYC"** section
4. Click **"Submit KYC Documents"**

### Step 2: Upload Documents

For each document:

1. **Select Document Type** from dropdown
2. **Upload File** (PDF/JPG/PNG)
3. **Verify** document is clear and readable
4. **Add Notes** if needed (e.g., "Company seal applied")

**Document Upload Checklist:**

- [ ] Identity Proof (PAN Card)
- [ ] Address Proof (Aadhaar/Utility Bill)
- [ ] Bank Proof (Cancelled Cheque)
- [ ] Business Proof (GST/Incorporation Certificate)
- [ ] Website Screenshot (if no live site)

### Step 3: Verify Bank Account

**Two options:**

#### Option 1: Penny Drop Verification (Instant)
1. Razorpay sends ₹1 to your account
2. Verify the reference code
3. Account verified instantly

#### Option 2: Bank Statement Upload
1. Upload last 3 months' bank statement
2. Manual verification (takes 1-2 days)

**Recommended:** Option 1 (Penny Drop) for faster verification

### Step 4: Submit for Review

1. Review all entered information
2. Check for typos or errors
3. Click **"Submit for KYC Review"**
4. You'll receive a confirmation email

**Expected Timeline:**
- ✅ Initial review: 24-48 hours
- ✅ Additional verification: 2-5 business days
- ✅ Total: 3-7 business days

## ⚠️ Common Rejection Reasons

### 1. **Bank Account Name Mismatch**
**Problem:** Account holder name doesn't match PAN/business name

**Solution:**
- Ensure account name exactly matches PAN card
- For Pvt Ltd: Use company current account (not personal)
- For Proprietorship: Use account with same name as on PAN

### 2. **Poor Quality Documents**
**Problem:** Documents are blurred, cut-off, or unreadable

**Solution:**
- Scan documents in high resolution (300 DPI minimum)
- Ensure all corners are visible
- Use PDF format for multi-page documents
- Don't use mobile photos (use scanner app)

### 3. **Business Type Mismatch**
**Problem:** Business description doesn't match selected category

**Solution:**
- Clearly describe it as SaaS/subscription platform
- Match description with category selection
- Provide website link showing subscription plans

### 4. **Incomplete Documentation**
**Problem:** Missing mandatory documents

**Solution:**
- For Pvt Ltd: Include MOA, AOA, Board Resolution
- For all: PAN + Address Proof + Bank Proof mandatory
- Upload GST certificate if registered

### 5. **Website/Product Not Live**
**Problem:** Website is down or doesn't show pricing

**Solution:**
- Ensure website is accessible 24/7
- Show clear pricing on website
- Have contact information visible
- If no website: Provide detailed business plan

### 6. **Suspicious Business Activity**
**Problem:** Business category flagged as high-risk

**Solution:**
- Clearly explain your business model
- Provide customer testimonials/traction
- Show legitimate business operations
- Avoid words like "crypto", "gambling", "adult content"

### 7. **Address Proof Issues**
**Problem:** Address on proof doesn't match business address

**Solution:**
- Use same address on all documents
- If home-based: Mention "Home Office" in description
- Provide utility bill in same name

## ✅ Post-Approval Checklist

Once KYC is approved, you'll receive an email. Follow these steps:

### 1. Get Live API Keys

1. Go to Settings → API Keys
2. Click **"Generate Live Key"**
3. Copy **Live Key ID** (starts with `rzp_live_`)
4. Copy **Live Key Secret** (keep secure!)

### 2. Update Environment Variables

Update your project with live keys:

**Frontend (.env):**
```env
VITE_RAZORPAY_KEY_ID=rzp_live_xxxxxxxxxxxxx
```

**Backend (Firebase Secrets):**
```bash
firebase functions:secrets:set RAZORPAY_KEY_ID
# Paste: rzp_live_xxxxxxxxxxxxx

firebase functions:secrets:set RAZORPAY_KEY_SECRET
# Paste: your live key secret
```

### 3. Create Live Subscription Plans

Recreate all 8 plans in **Live Mode**:
- Go to Products → Subscriptions → Plans
- Switch to **"Live Mode"** toggle (top-right)
- Create same 8 plans with same Plan IDs
- Copy plan IDs and verify they match

### 4. Update Webhook for Live Mode

1. Go to Settings → Webhooks
2. Switch to **Live Mode**
3. Add new webhook with your production URL
4. Select all 8 events
5. Copy new Webhook Secret
6. Update Firebase secret:
   ```bash
   firebase functions:secrets:set RAZORPAY_WEBHOOK_SECRET
   # Paste: whsec_live_xxxxxxxxxxxxx
   ```

### 5. Deploy with Live Keys

```bash
# Deploy functions with live secrets
firebase deploy --only functions

# Deploy frontend with live key
npm run build
firebase deploy --only hosting
```

### 6. Test with Real Card

⚠️ **Use small amount for first test** (e.g., monthly plan)

1. Go to your live website
2. Select a plan
3. Use your own credit/debit card
4. Complete payment
5. Verify:
   - Payment shows in Razorpay Dashboard
   - Subscription activated
   - Credits updated
   - Transaction recorded

### 7. Set Up Alerts & Monitoring

1. **Email Alerts:**
   - Settings → Notifications
   - Enable payment success/failure emails

2. **Webhook Monitoring:**
   - Check Firebase Functions logs regularly
   - Set up error alerts

3. **Payment Monitoring:**
   - Monitor Razorpay Dashboard daily
   - Check for failed payments
   - Respond to customer payment issues

Prepare everything before starting. Don't upload partial documents.
